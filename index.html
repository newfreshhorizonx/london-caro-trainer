Source and editing guide: github.com/newfreshhorizonx/london-caro-trainer. Learn shows arrows and notes; Drill hides them and counts mistakes (3 clean runs in a row = mastered). A good move in the wrong order, or another engine-approved move, isn't counted as a mistake: you get a yellow note and the trainer steers you back to the repertoire move. Three repertoires: the London as White, the Caro-Kann against 1.e4, and the King's Indian against 1.d4. Where IM Alex Banzea has a clear choice in his own games (chess.com: alex_banzea), the lines follow him; the rest is mainstream theory, checked with Stockfish. Each line is quizzed to roughly move 10–15; after that you can step through the ideas to around move 20 without being tested. Progress is saved in this browser. Keys: R restart · H hint · N next.
